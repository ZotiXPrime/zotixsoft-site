About Burst League

Arena soccer with anime heroes instead of cars

Burst League is an anime hero soccer game for PC. Two teams, one giant ball and a walled arena, but you play on foot as a hero who can run, double jump and fly. Hit the ball with your blade, your ki or your whole body and put it in the other goal.

Sports meets MOBA

Every hero has health, a three-hit blade combo, a ki shot that charges into a full beam, and one signature skill on a cooldown. Knock an opponent out and their team plays short until they respawn.

Eight heroes from Emerald Burst

Kyro, Master Ren, Hana, Lord Vael, Raikiri, Luna, Grunkle and Elder Oren each bring an element and a skill: blink to the ball, stun a cone of enemies, heal up, pull the ball with a void grip, freeze attackers or shield the whole team.

Boost, fly and go supersonic

Tap to dash, hold to boost-run, and thrust through the air toward wherever you aim. Boost pads refill the tank. Charge your meter and trigger Emerald Burst for more speed, more damage and a shockwave.

Power-ups, barriers and big saves

Grab orbs for Mega Beam, Rapid Fire, Titan, Overdrive or a full heal. Lock your own goal with a barrier that bounces shots back. The game calls out saves, epic saves, clears and assists, and names an MVP.

Make the hero yours

A locker of colourways, outfits, blades, hair, toppers and jet boots, plus 16 achievements to unlock. Play with keyboard and mouse or a controller.

Burst League FAQ

What is Burst League?

Burst League is an arena soccer game where anime heroes replace the cars. You run, fly and boost around a walled pitch, hitting a giant ball with a blade, ki blasts and hero skills to score.

What platforms is Burst League on?

Burst League is a Windows PC game. It is coming soon; the release date has not been announced yet.

How many heroes are in Burst League?

Eight: Kyro, Master Ren, Hana, Lord Vael, Raikiri, Luna, Grunkle and Elder Oren. Each has an element, a role and a signature skill.

Is Burst League like Rocket League?

It comes from the same idea of arena soccer with boost and aerials, but you play as a flying hero with health, attacks and abilities instead of driving a car, so it plays closer to a sports game crossed with a hero brawler.

Is Burst League connected to Emerald Burst?

Yes. It stars Kyro and the cast of Emerald Burst, the ZOTIXSOFT action game, along with the Hourkey blade and the Emerald Burst transformation.

Does Burst League support controllers?

Yes. It plays with a gamepad or with keyboard and mouse.

Who made Burst League?

Burst League is made by Arvin Setareh at ZOTIXSOFT.
