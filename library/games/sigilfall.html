About SIGILFALL

A duel game with a pulse

A hand-shaped cursor, cards that slam onto the field and rivals who talk back every single turn. SIGILFALL plays like the classic PC duel games: 4000 life points, tribute summons, set and flip, spells and traps with a yes or no prompt.

Eight rivals, all voiced

Bram Cinder the smug chef, Lady Vesper and her mirrors, rich kid Rex Gilder, Rook Volt, the Moth Saint, Bramble Hex and the Hollow King, a boss with 6000 life points. Beat him to unlock a secret eighth duelist.

Open packs, build your deck

Every win earns a foil pack from Grandpa's Vault. Pull rares, grow your collection and build a 30 to 40 card deck of your own.

Made by one person

Designed and programmed by Arvin Setareh at ZotixSoft in C++ on SDL3, with an original soundtrack.
