What's in it

Career mode

Ten claims on the Claims Desk: hoop races over the rooftops, condemned buildings, a rental-car crusher, a pitch invasion, a moon heist and The Adjuster himself. Par times and par damages earn up to three stars each.

Power tiers

Every dollar of damage is XP. Rank up from Civil Nuisance to Heat Death: faster legs, bigger jumps, glowing hair, an aura, and from tier three, real flight.

Sky islands

Floating islands above the city, only reachable once you can fly. Hubcaps and power orbs on every one.

Comic shading

Ink outlines, halftone shadows and flat colour bands. The whole city looks like a printed comic, toggle with C.

Wear the traffic

Rip a sedan into steel mitts, a van into stompers, a bus into armour and a tank into a cannon. The Big Deductible beam lets you fly on the recoil.

Combos and juice

Chain wrecks for a combo payout, with screen shake, starburst flares, cash bursts and a rubber stamp that slams CLAIM APPROVED across the screen.
