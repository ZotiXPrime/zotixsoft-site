About FLEECED!

A cartoon stealth game about stealing sheep

FLEECED! is a funny 3D stealth platformer for PC. You play Slick, a fox burglar on a timer, and the geese of Goosewater are guarding every sheep in the valley. Sneak past patrols, grab the sheep and get them out before the clock hits zero.

Timed heists, more than one way in

Every level is a sheep heist you can crack your own way. Tiptoe through the shadows, lure guards with corn, bonk a goose by landing on its head, or vanish into a wool bomb cloud. Botched it? Pop a rewinder and try again.

Gadgets for a professional sheep thief

Vacuum sheep through jail bars, blow pie smells at jumpy sheep with the fan, jetpack them onto desert mesas, float them out on balloons and stroll past the guards in a goose disguise.

Seven hand-built levels

Moonlit Goosewater, Clover Gulch, Mutton Mesa, the Docks, a hedge-maze Manor, icy Frostbite and Fort Foie Gras, each with golden corn, golden eggs, a best time to beat and hidden easter eggs.

Silly, never gross

Sheriff Gander goes blind mid-sip, the deputies gossip in chirpy subtitles and never look up, and a hooded goose will sell you $MUTTON with no refunds. Easy to pick up, with forgiving jumps and full controller support.

FLEECED! FAQ

What is FLEECED!?

FLEECED! is a cartoon 3D stealth platformer where you play Slick the fox and steal sheep from goose guards in timed heist levels. It is a comedy game: sneak, distract, bonk and gadget your way out with the flock.

What platforms is FLEECED! on?

FLEECED! is a Windows PC game. It is coming soon; the release date has not been announced yet.

How many levels does FLEECED! have?

Seven: Goosewater, Clover Gulch, Mutton Mesa, the Docks, the Manor, Frostbite and Fort Foie Gras. There are also 24 trophies, golden corn and golden eggs to collect on every level.

Does FLEECED! support controllers?

Yes. It has full gamepad support with on-screen button prompts that switch automatically, plus keyboard and mouse.

Is FLEECED! family friendly?

The humour is cartoon slapstick: silly, not gross, with no gore. No sheep are harmed. The sheriff goose does have a suspicious flask.

Is FLEECED! like the old sheep-stealing stealth games?

It is an original game in the spirit of PS1-era cartoon sheep-heist games such as Sheep Raider, with its own characters, levels and gadgets.

Who made FLEECED!?

FLEECED! is made by Arvin Setareh at ZOTIXSOFT on an in-house C++ and SDL3 engine.
