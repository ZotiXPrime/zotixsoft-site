Arvin Setareh

Arvin Setareh is an independent game developer and software engineer based in California, and the founder of ZotixSoft. He builds games in C++, Unreal Engine 5, Unreal Editor for Fortnite and Roblox, and teaches Linux.

Emerald Burst

Arvin Setareh's debut commercial game, Emerald Burst, launched on the Epic Games Store on October 23, 2026 as an Epic First Run title. It's a 3D action adventure with combo combat, two transformations, seven bosses and an original soundtrack composed by Arvin Setareh. The game is written in C++ with raylib and integrates Epic Online Services for login and achievements.
