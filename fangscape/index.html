The Hunger

You wake in your coffin. The sun is setting. You're hungry.

Fangscape is a love letter to the click-to-move MMOs of the early 2000s: 0.6-second ticks, right-click menus, a chatbox full of level-ups, and a bank that's always too full. The twist is that you're the monster.

Feed on villagers to heal. Fill vials with blood. Duck the vampire hunters who come looking when the village gets suspicious. Then go and take on goblins, swamp gators, a pharaoh, a fire titan and, eventually, an elder dragon.

☀
Mind the daylightThe sun won't burn you, but outdoors in daylight you hit 25% weaker and miss more. Hunt at night, or fight indoors.
What's in the coffin

Everything you loved about old-school RPGs, with fangs.

Slow, satisfying progression on a big handmade map. No microtransactions, no account, no server. Just you and the grind.

Tick-based combat

Classic click-to-fight with combat styles, special attack bars and real gear progression through seven original metals up to Mythic Blood.

Melee, ranged & magic

Blades, mauls, whips and claws. Bows from Shortbow to the Emerald Jade Bow. Staves for blood magic. Switch whenever you like.

Blood Demon Arts

A 23-spell book of blood bolts, curses and fire, ice, earth, storm and void incantations, each with its own look. Autocast and go.

Juicy special attacks

Shadow clones, bat swarms, chain lightning, frost shatter, blood tides. Almost 30 spec weapons, and every one of them hits like a truck.

Skill it up

Mine, smelt and smith your own gear. Chop, fish, cook, light fires, bury bones for Incantations and harvest blood. Fifteen skills to max.

Capes that slap

Legendary capes that burn, drip lava, ripple like water, crackle with frost or glitter with stars. Plus god shields, robes and coloured boots.
